# App Review Reply - In-App Purchase Locations

Hello,

Thank you for continuing the review. The In-App Purchases are available in the iOS app through the following paths.

## Fastest Path From First Screen

1. Launch the app on iPhone or iPad.
2. On the first FamilyBridge screen, locate the section titled **In-App Purchases for App Review**.
3. Tap one of the following:
   - **Family Subscription** - opens the FIIS Support family subscription purchase screen.
   - **Provider Subscription** - opens the provider subscription purchase screen.
   - **Guidance Window** - opens the Professional Guidance Window one-time in-app purchase screen.

## Alternate Path Through Demo Mode

1. Launch the app.
2. Tap **Full Feature Demo Index** or open **Reviewer Demo**.
3. On the **Review Mode** tab, scroll to **In-App Purchase Review Paths**.
4. Tap **Family Subscription**, **Provider Subscription**, or **Guidance Window**.

## Authenticated Purchase Path

If the App Store purchase button asks the reviewer to sign in first:

1. Tap **Sign In or Create Account**.
2. Sign in with the App Review demo account provided in App Review Information.
3. Return to the first screen or open:
   - `/family-purchase`
   - `/provider-purchase`
   - `/moderator-purchase`
4. The purchase screen will display the Apple sandbox In-App Purchase button.

## Products Available For Review

- Family Subscription: `com.familybridgeapp.app.family_monthly`
- Provider Monthly Subscription: `com.familybridgeapp.app.provider_monthly_v2`
- Provider Quarterly Subscription: `com.familybridgeapp.app.provider_quarterly_v2`
- Professional Guidance Window: `com.familybridgeapp.app.crisis_moderation_daily`

These products are configured for Apple sandbox review through RevenueCat. The app does not use web checkout for these iOS purchase paths.
