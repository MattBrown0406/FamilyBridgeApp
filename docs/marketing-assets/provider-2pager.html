For Treatment Providers

The family system, finally visible — and actionable.

FamilyBridge gives clinical teams a real-time view of what's happening inside the family while a loved one is in or after treatment — and gives families the structure, coaching, and accountability that protects your clinical work.

Pattern Detection · Boundary Coaching · Outcome Predictions

Why family-side blind spots stall outcomes.

What you do inside the building is half the story. The enabling, the contradictions, the conversations that erode boundaries at home — that's the other half, and until now you've had no structured signal into it. FamilyBridge turns the family system into observable, supportable, measurable data, without asking the family to do clinical work they aren't trained for.

Six modules. Three layers. One platform.
The Full System
FIIS™ Pattern Detection
Surfaces emotional, behavioral, and recovery patterns that fly under the radar between sessions.
Outcome Predictions
Probabilistic risk modeling that flags shifts before they show up in a session.
Intervention Readiness
A 0–100 score grounded in observable behavior, plus execution planning and real-time conversation coaching.
Accountability Engine
Behavioral contracts the whole family — and the provider team — can see, with performance tracking on both sides.
AI Learning Layer
Privacy-preserving, evidence-proportional adaptation. The platform gets smarter without becoming a black box.
Input Reconciliation
Catches contradictions between family members, prompts for clarity, and assigns confidence to every data point.

Who this is built for

Interventionists, residential and PHP/IOP programs, sober living operators, family-program clinicians, and aftercare coordinators who need to extend their clinical reach into the home without adding staff hours.

What it does · How it works · How to start

Extend your clinical reach into the home — without adding staff.

FamilyBridge is the layer that makes the family-side of recovery visible, structured, and supportable. Your team keeps doing what they do best. We carry the load between sessions.

Catch the slip before the call comes in.

Pattern detection and trajectory tracking surface emotional and behavioral shifts days before they become a phone call from a panicked parent.

End "he said / she said."

Input Reconciliation flags contradictions between family members and prompts for clarity, so your team isn't refereeing the family system blind.

Hold families to their commitments.

Behavioral contracts and accountability scoring make boundary work visible. Enabling stops being invisible labor — it becomes a measurable variable.

Document the family-side work.

Aftercare plans, HIPAA releases, medication compliance, financial coordination, care transitions — one record, not seven sticky notes.

From intervention to aftercare, three steps.
How It Works
1

Family is invited

Your team — or the family themselves — creates a Family Group in minutes. Roles are assigned: spouse, parent, sibling, provider observer.

2

System listens & coaches

Daily check-ins, document uploads, communication coaching, and boundary work feed the FIIS™ engine. Patterns surface; scores update.

3

You see what matters

Provider-side dashboards show readiness, risk, contradiction flags, and accountability — so your sessions start grounded in real signal.

Built on doctrine you can defend

FamilyBridge is a coaching and pattern-recognition platform — not a clinician, not a lawyer, not an emergency responder. No diagnosis. No treatment prescriptions. No legal advice. Crisis escalations route to 911 first, then to the family's designated moderator or interventionist. Every AI adaptation is volatility-aware, privacy-safe, transparent, and reversible.
